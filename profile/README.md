## What Is MAXSURF Modeler

MAXSURF Modeler is the hull-geometry seat of Bentley’s MAXSURF naval-architecture suite. Offices open it when a contract already exists: a ferry, a patrol craft, an offshore support hull, a large motor yacht. MAXSURF Modeler marine hull is the NURBS (or equivalent marine surface) definition. MAXSURF Modeler design offsets are what leave toward the yard. MAXSURF Modeler naval architecture here means the start of a calculation chain the rest of MAXSURF continues - resistance, stability, motions - on geometry you administer.

This is a different scale from DELFTship’s small-craft desk and a different job from ShipConstructor’s production structure. Modeler does not nest plates or write NC for a burning machine. It makes a hull that analysis and class can believe. Teams keep templates, layer standards, and naming for the office, not a pile of untitled files.

Use it on vessels you are employed or contracted to design. Keep client geometry inside that job. A copied hull from another tender is not yours to reuse unless the contract says so.
